## Debrief

**Informatics principle:** Correct identification does not prove correct routing.

The barcode resolved to the right case, but a procedure-to-work-area mapping sent it to the wrong queue. The missing gross-room handoff delayed processing while the audit trail preserved enough evidence to diagnose the defect.

LIS and operations teams repair and test routing. The pathologist approves clinical workflow meaning and the safe recovery of affected specimens.
