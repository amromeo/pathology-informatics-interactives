# Trace an order and a specimen at your institution

## Who to meet

Meet an accessioning supervisor and an LIS analyst.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Ask the supervisor to choose an accession you are authorized to review. Start with its requested examination, specimen requirements, accession and container or part identifiers.
2. Have the analyst show you which procedure entry supplies the worklist destination. Record the entry's name, its relevant setting and how the team identifies its version.
3. Follow the available receipt and handoff events. For each one, identify who or what recorded it, the location and time, and the action it establishes.
4. Ask how staff handle an unrecognized procedure and an undocumented handoff. Review how a proposed routing change is tested before use.
5. Compare the records for one AP and one CP workflow. Identify which links they share and which records are specific to their work.

## Bring back

Bring a one-page diagram connecting the order, specimen, procedure entry and receiving worklist. Distinguish physical receipt events from software assignments. Include one unresolved question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

Which record would you use to find the last documented location? Which cases would you include when testing a correction to one route?

## Privacy

Use records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep patient identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a diagram without patient identifiers.
