# Apply it at your institution

## Who to meet

Meet an accessioning supervisor and LIS analyst.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Trace the order catalog entry to the worklist.
2. With your supervisor, select one approved record or workflow to examine.
3. Draw an order-to-specimen-to-worklist trace, comparing one AP and one CP workflow.
4. Compare the proposed change with local policy and identify who would test it and who would approve it.

## Bring back

Bring a one-page annotated diagram, table or checklist that documents this task: draw an order-to-specimen-to-worklist trace, comparing one AP and one CP workflow. Include one unresolved question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

What observation would change your conclusion? Explain why the shortcut “reroute every specimen to the same bench” could mislead the team.

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a workflow description without patient identifiers.

## Further local discussion

#### Local practicum

Follow one AP or CP specimen from receipt to final report. Record each human or device interaction, barcode event, location timestamp, automated routing decision, exception, and recovery control.
