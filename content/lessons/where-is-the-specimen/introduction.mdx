# Where Is the Specimen?

> **Synthetic case:** At 08:40 on Tuesday, a pathologists’ assistant notices that a tissue excision on the operating-room delivery list is absent from the gross-room worklist. Accession S26-1042 appears on the cytology worklist instead.

## Case setup

You are the pathology resident working with the surgical pathology supervisor and an LIS analyst. Accessioning staff received container C-1042-A at 08:11 and verified its patient and part against the requisition. The supervisor asks you to establish what the records show, find why the case was assigned to cytology, and recommend both specimen recovery and a configuration correction.

On Monday, the LIS team added a procedure entry for tissue excisions by copying an existing entry. The change review included routine biopsy and cytology examples but omitted a test of the newly added excision code. The approved destination for tissue excisions remained the gross room.

## Your task

Open the timestamped records before deciding where staff last documented the container. Compare the routing configuration with the approved destinations, edit the entries that need correction, and test the reported and unaffected procedures. Then choose how staff should recover the existing specimen.

## Key point

Identification answers **what is this?** Tracking answers **where has it been?** Routing answers **where should it go next?** A barcode can support all three, but configuration determines the action.
