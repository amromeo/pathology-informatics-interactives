# Where Is the Specimen?

> **Synthetic case:** A surgical specimen has a valid barcode and correct patient match, yet appears in the cytology queue instead of the gross room.

## Your task

Reconstruct the digital trail, distinguish tracking from routing, and determine how a correctly identified container can still move through the wrong workflow.

## Concept lens

Identification answers **what is this?** Tracking answers **where has it been?** Routing answers **where should it go next?** A barcode can support all three, but configuration determines the action.
