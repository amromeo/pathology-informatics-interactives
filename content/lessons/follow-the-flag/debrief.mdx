## Debrief

**Informatics principle:** Successful transmission is not the same as clinically correct display.

The local `CH` code retained meaning inside the analyzer and LIS but was not translated into the receiver’s `HH` convention. The EHR displayed the number while losing critical severity.

The integration team implements mappings. Laboratory and EHR clinical owners approve meaning and presentation. Validation must include every abnormality category and unaffected behavior.
