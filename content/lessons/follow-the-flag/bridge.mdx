### Review more than the incident

The six replay records are selected category checks, not a patient population
or an estimate of the interface failure rate. The corrected mapping should
preserve both critical directions, routine high and low, and normal results.
The unfamiliar XH category should produce an exception requiring investigation.

Before release, record expected and observed displays, the mapping revision,
the approver, and how to restore the previous version. Review the affected time
period for already transmitted results, reconcile missing indicators, and
document necessary communications. Monitor unknown-category logs after release.
A test that checks only that messages were accepted would miss this defect.
