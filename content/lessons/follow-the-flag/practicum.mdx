# Follow one result message at your institution

## Who to meet

An interface analyst.

## Time

30–60 minutes.

## Steps

1. With your supervisor, choose one result message you are allowed to review.
2. Mark up the message: find the patient identifier, the order and accession numbers, the test identifier, the value, the unit, the status and the flag.
3. Open the same result in the LIS and in the EHR report clinicians use. Note anything that looks different, especially flags, comments and units.
4. Ask the analyst where the flag translation table is kept, who tests a change to it and who approves it.

## Bring back

The marked-up message beside the two screens, with each field matched to where it appears. Include one open question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

What would you need to see to believe a flag had come through correctly? Why might sending the message again, without looking at its fields, mislead the team?

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor allows you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots in the hospital's own systems, and bring back a description with no patient identifiers.
