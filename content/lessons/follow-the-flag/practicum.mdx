# Apply it at your institution

## Who to meet

Meet an interface analyst.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Compare the source message with the received report.
2. With your supervisor, select one approved record or workflow to examine.
3. Annotate one approved message with identifiers, status and flag fields, then compare its chart display.
4. Compare the proposed change with local policy and identify who would test it and who would approve it.

## Bring back

Bring a one-page annotated diagram, table or checklist that documents this task: annotate one approved message with identifiers, status and flag fields, then compare its chart display. Include one unresolved question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

What observation would change your conclusion? Explain why the shortcut “repeat the transmission without inspecting fields” could mislead the team.

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a workflow description without patient identifiers.
