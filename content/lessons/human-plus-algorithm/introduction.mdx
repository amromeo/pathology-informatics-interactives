# Reviewing a Mitosis Detection Tool

You are the pathology resident helping Dr. Rao, a breast pathologist, evaluate a tool that detects mitoses.

The tool marks possible mitotic figures within a chosen tumor area. The pathologist has to check those marks, search the rest of the area and record the final count. The tool does not give a diagnosis, a grade or a prognosis.

The vendor's sensitivity figure does not tell Dr. Rao how many false marks someone will have to check, or what happens when a reader sees no marks at all. You will look at the marked locations, change the marking threshold, compare the errors at two sites, and review a separate study of pathologists using the tool and a monitoring report.

The list has 12 locations picked from a development review: five that are mitotic figures and seven that are not. Two pathologists agreed the right answer for each one. All 12 stay in the exercise. This small, hand-picked set is for working through the arithmetic and looking at errors; it does not estimate how accurate the tool is on whole slides in practice.

## What you will be able to do

- Evaluate the pathologist and the tool working together, not just the tool.
- Review the whole design of a study of readers using the tool.
- Explain why the share of correct marks on a hand-picked list is not the tool's clinical sensitivity.
