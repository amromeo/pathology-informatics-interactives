# Twelve Hours Offline

> **Synthetic tabletop:** Construction severs network access across a Level I trauma center. Orders, registration, results, and identifiers no longer cross hospital systems.

## Your task

Choose actions for activation, sustained operations, and recovery. Then inspect the identity, critical-result, duplicate-order, and complete-result checks that medical leadership needs before normal operation resumes. The aim is not merely to keep testing—it is to preserve identity and a trustworthy longitudinal record.

## Key point

Downtime has at least three phases: controlled activation, safe degraded operation, and high-risk recovery. Restoration of connectivity does not complete reconciliation.
