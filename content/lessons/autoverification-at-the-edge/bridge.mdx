### What the complete replay establishes

The 22 records are a selected validation set, not a sample for estimating the
laboratory's daily hold rate. They include the reported increase, exact and nearby
boundaries, a decrease, stable results, missing or incompatible history, QC and
instrument exceptions, and ineligible result statuses. Expand a record to inspect
why its expected decision matters.

Validation must demonstrate **correct release as well as correct holds**. Holding
every result would make some errors less visible while delaying routine reports.
After a change, repeat the complete set and compare expected with observed actions.
The test record belongs with the rule revision, approver, implementation date,
and procedure for restoring the prior version if needed.

Before use, the middleware analyst verifies the rule in the test environment,
including how held results appear on the technologist's worklist and how released
results appear in the LIS and EHR. The laboratory director reviews the clinical
criteria with chemistry staff. After use begins, review unexpected releases,
reasons for holds, manual overrides, and reporting delays. A high automatic-release
percentage alone cannot establish that the rule is correct.
