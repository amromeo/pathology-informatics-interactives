# Autoverification at the Edge

> **Synthetic case:** A troponin result rises from 12 to 48 ng/L, passes QC and instrument checks, and autoverifies despite a clinically important delta.

## Your task

Inspect the complete rule: analytical flags, QC, reference and critical limits, absolute and relative deltas, result status, missing history, and exception handling. Repair the category, not just the incident.

## Concept lens

Autoverification is an approved decision system. Analytical validity is necessary but does not satisfy every patient-result release criterion.
