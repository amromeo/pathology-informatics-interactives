# Review one local automatic-release rule

## Who to meet

Meet a chemistry supervisor and the analyst who maintains the LIS or middleware rule.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Select an approved rule with the supervisor. Identify the order type, assay and population to which it applies.
2. Read the written criteria beside the configured conditions. Ask the analyst to show how the program combines them.
3. Follow one held and one released result through the checks. Identify each input and its source.
4. Review the validation records for boundaries, missing history, instrument flags and a result that should continue to release. Include a missing-input example if the rule calculates another result.
5. Ask who approves a change, where it is tested, how staff monitor its behavior and how they would stop or reverse it.

## Bring back

Bring a diagram of the checks and a small table of expected hold/release decisions. Identify the saved rule version and the reason each example is in the test set.

## Discuss with faculty

Which condition would you test on its own? How would you detect unnecessary holds as well as unexpected releases?

## Privacy

Use records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep patient identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a description without patient identifiers.
