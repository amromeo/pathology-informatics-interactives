# Apply it at your institution

## Who to meet

Meet a chemistry supervisor and middleware analyst.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Compare the rule with the approved order pathway.
2. With your supervisor, select one approved record or workflow to examine.
3. Build a release/hold matrix including missing history, missing calculation inputs and an acceptable result.
4. Compare the proposed change with local policy and identify who would test it and who would approve it.

## Bring back

Bring a one-page annotated diagram, table or checklist that documents this task: build a release/hold matrix including missing history, missing calculation inputs and an acceptable result. Include one unresolved question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

What observation would change your conclusion? Explain why the shortcut “change the threshold until fewer cases hold” could mislead the team.

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a workflow description without patient identifiers.

## Further local discussion

#### Local practicum

Under supervision, review one local autoverification rule. Document its criteria, development and approval history, exception workflow, validation set, monitoring, and most recent controlled change.
