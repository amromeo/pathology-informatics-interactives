# Follow one test change at your institution

## Who to meet

A section director and the LIS build analyst.

## Time

30–60 minutes.

## Steps

1. With faculty, choose a test that was recently added or changed. Follow it from the request to the approved specification and the exact settings now live. Note who supplied each fact and who approved what the results mean.
2. Compare the order, specimen requirements, results, allowed answers, units and reference information, comments, statuses and codes across the laboratory procedure, the LIS and the official EHR report.
3. Pick one normal case and two exceptions that could show up a setting copied by mistake.
4. Look at the change record, evidence that the report was checked in the EHR, procedures, training, the downtime plan and the checks done after go-live.

## Bring back

A short list of any gaps, each with the person who should deal with it and how it would be checked. Include one open question and the person who can answer it. Talk the list through with the laboratory team before proposing any change to the live system.

## Discuss with faculty

Which setting is most likely to be copied by mistake from a similar test? Why does copying a similar test without a specification cause problems?

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor allows you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots in the hospital's own systems, and bring back a description with no patient identifiers.
