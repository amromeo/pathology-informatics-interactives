## Debrief

**Informatics principle:** High-reliability builds separate request, vetting, configuration, testing, approval, deployment, and monitoring.

Concatenated results and unapproved visibility rules obscured clinical meaning and made downstream reuse unreliable. FDA authorization of the assay does not define the local information-system build or eliminate verification.

Analysts configure and test. Technical staff validate laboratory performance. Pathologists approve names, fields, interpretation, display, and clinical release.
