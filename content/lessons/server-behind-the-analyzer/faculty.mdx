# Faculty guide: The Server Behind the Analyzer

Do not allow the discussion to collapse into password advice. Ask how each finding affects confidentiality, integrity, availability, or more than one.

The two incomplete installation plans each correct one real problem. Ask learners why protected hosting passes the environmental check but not the backup-restoration check, and why controlled vendor sessions do not correct shared local access or a flat network. Include business associate agreements and vendor-access terms where appropriate to the local arrangement.
