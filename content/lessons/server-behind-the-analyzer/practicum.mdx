# Local practicum

With the laboratory manager or LIS team, identify one system that stores or routes laboratory orders or results outside the main laboratory information system (LIS).

1. Record where the system is physically or virtually hosted and who is responsible for its power, cooling, operating system, and network connection.
2. Identify which laboratory staff, IT staff, and vendor personnel can sign in. Determine whether they use named accounts and whether access is limited to the work each person performs.
3. Ask how vendor support sessions are approved, ended, and reviewed.
4. Find the date of the most recent backup-restoration test. Record what service and data were restored and how the laboratory confirmed that queued orders and results remained correct.
5. Identify the laboratory leader who reviews these controls before installation or a major change.

Do not record passwords, network addresses, patient information, or other security-sensitive details in teaching notes. Follow local policy and perform the review under appropriate supervision.
