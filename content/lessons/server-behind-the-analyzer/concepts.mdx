### Ask three questions about every finding

First ask who could see patient information that they should not see. Protecting information from unauthorized disclosure is called **confidentiality**. The unrestricted shared login and persistent vendor connection both raise that question for orders, results, and patient identifiers.

Next ask whether an unauthorized or unattributed person could change a routing rule, message queue, result, or recovery file. Protecting information and configuration from improper change is called **integrity**.

Finally ask whether the middleware will be available when the laboratory needs to route orders and results. Protecting dependable access to the service is called **availability**. Heat, power loss, ransomware, and an unusable backup can all interrupt patient-care work.

One finding may threaten more than one property, and one control rarely addresses every finding. The portfolio exercise therefore asks what the laboratory can implement now, what risk remains, and what must be completed before approval.
