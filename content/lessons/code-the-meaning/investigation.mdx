### Other standards solve other parts of the report

An **orderable** is something a clinician can request. A panel order may cause
several observations to be performed and reported. An **observation** identifies
what was measured or assessed. Its **value** is the answer, and its **unit**
expresses the quantity when applicable. A report assembles those observations
with interpretation and context.

For example, an estrogen-receptor observation, its percentage result, the %
unit, and the pathologist's interpretation are separate data elements. The
breast panel order does not supply all of those meanings. A billing entry
documents a service for administrative use; it cannot establish what a
particular tumor's result was.

### Match each standard to the actual task

**LOINC** identifies observations and related clinical information. **SNOMED
CT** represents detailed clinical concepts, including findings. **UCUM** gives
units a machine-readable representation. These can work together in one
record; choosing one does not eliminate the other elements.

**ICD** classifications support diagnosis classification for specified
administrative and reporting uses. **CPT** represents services in the US
billing workflow named here. The required edition, documented service, and
submission rules matter; this exercise does not assign a billable code.

**DICOM** describes medical imaging objects and associated metadata, including
whole-slide microscopy. **CAP cancer protocols** specify structured reporting
elements for the relevant cancer and procedure. Neither an image format nor a
report template is a universal clinical vocabulary. Report elements may use
terminology codes within their structure.

A short HER2 label does not establish the specimen, method, or result
scale. An immunostain interpretation and a gene-amplification assessment cannot
be merged solely because their labels contain HER2. If the source catalog
lacks the needed definition, obtain it from the assay owner before accepting
a mapping. Deferring an unsupported mapping is a completed review decision.

### Document what the mapping asserts

Record the local identifier and definition, target system and code, terminology
version, relationship, reviewer, date, and intended consumer. A broader or
narrower concept may be useful for a specified purpose, but it is not
automatically an exact equivalent. Do not infer that two local assays are
interchangeable merely because they share a LOINC code; analytical comparability
requires separate evidence.

Test representative values, units, interpretation categories, and missing
fields at the destination. A valid code can still produce an invalid record
when combined with the wrong value, specimen, or unit.
