## Local practicum

With the LIS analyst or terminology specialist, choose one numeric result from an approved local catalog. Record its local identifier, specimen, property, scale, time, method, and unit. Find a candidate on the LOINC website and compare all six dimensions. Record the term status, release, lookup date, code, and rationale; identify the source documentation for the local definition.

Have an assay owner review the proposal. Include one plausible alternative and explain why it does not fit. If the definition is incomplete, specify what must be obtained before approval. Describe how you would validate the receiving display and investigate previously transmitted results if the current mapping were wrong. Do not change a production mapping as part of this exercise.
