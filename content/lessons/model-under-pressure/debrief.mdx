## Debrief

**Informatics principle:** Vendor performance does not establish local clinical performance.

The local scanner, stain characteristics, and small-lesion subgroup were inadequately represented. The immediate problem is a generalizability failure with subgroup brittleness; later workflow changes could add drift.

The AI implementation and digital pathology teams conduct verification and monitoring. Pathology governance approves intended use, thresholds, human review, and deployment.
