# Faculty guide: Model Under Pressure

Require precise failure-language. “AI error” is not enough. Learners should separate a deficit present at initial transfer from drift over time and should recognize how a confident negative overlay can alter human behavior.

## Preparing discussion with API slides

Use [Association for Pathology Informatics teaching slides: Artificial Intelligence in Pathology (PowerPoint)](https://www.pathologyinformatics.org/assets/lms/jimharrison/PathInformatics%206%20AI%20slides.pptx) by James H. Harrison, Jr., slides 33–36, 43, 53, to prepare the discussion after residents have explained their case decision. Ask: Compare verification at a new site with monitoring after implementation. Identify which local scanner or staining differences need investigation before relying on the vendor's performance estimate. Have residents support their answer with a specific record or result from this lesson before comparing it with the slides.

These slides provide teaching background. Use the lesson's primary sources and current local policies for clinical criteria, legal requirements, and approval decisions; do not turn a slide example or numerical recommendation into a local requirement. If the PowerPoint link changes, locate the session on the [API teaching-slide page](https://www.pathologyinformatics.org/teaching-slide-sets). The lesson and discussion remain usable without the external file.
