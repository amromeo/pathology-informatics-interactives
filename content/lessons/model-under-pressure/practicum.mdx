# Review a proposed tool with your supervisor

## Who to meet

Meet a supervising pathologist and the person testing a model your laboratory is considering.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes. Ask to see the proposal and any local test results in advance.

## Steps

1. Ask what the pathologist will do with the model's output. Will it mark areas for review, sort cases or write text?
2. Find out how the training examples were labeled. Ask who makes the reference diagnoses for the local test and whether they can see the model's answers.
3. List the local scanners and preparation methods. Look for results separated by those groups and by a clinically important finding, such as small lesions.
4. Ask which result would prevent the laboratory from starting and which later finding would make it stop.
5. Find who keeps the model version record, who reviews misses and who checks the product's device status.

## Bring back

Prepare a one-page table with a row for each scanner or specimen group the laboratory needs to check. Include the number tested, the number detected and any missing comparison. Below it, name the person who reviews failures and the person who can pause use.

## Discuss with faculty

Which group might look acceptable when pooled with the others but perform poorly on its own? Show how your table would reveal that problem.

## Privacy

Review the proposal and slides in the systems your supervisor approves. Bring back counts and job titles without patient identifiers or confidential vendor material.
