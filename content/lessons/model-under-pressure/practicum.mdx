## Local practicum: review one proposed model

### Who to meet

A supervising pathologist and the person responsible for evaluating a proposed model.

### Time

30–60 minutes.

### Steps

1. State the intended patient population, specimen and clinical action.
2. Identify the model type and reference labels.
3. List local scanners, preparation methods and clinically important subgroups.
4. Find the proposed acceptance and pause criteria.
5. Identify the responsible reviewer, version record and device-status review.

### Bring back

A one-page verification plan with denominators, subgroup checks and named responsibilities.

### Discuss with faculty

Which failure would be concealed by a pooled rate, and what would trigger a pause?

### Privacy

Use approved documents and do not copy patient records or confidential vendor material into the plan.
