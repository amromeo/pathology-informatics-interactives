### What did the detector miss?

Here, **sensitivity** is the fraction of slides with metastases that the detector found. Divide detected positive slides by all slides known to be positive. These 50 local slides are all positive. They cannot tell you how often the detector marks a negative slide.

The detector did poorly as soon as it was tested locally. That is a **generalizability** problem: it did not work as well when moved to another setting. Compare the scanners and lesion sizes separately to see where the misses occurred.

A later change in the images or the detector's performance is called **drift**. The September results follow a stain change. That gives the laboratory something to investigate, but the timing alone does not tell you what caused the decline.

A model is **brittle** when a small change to an input, such as a slight color shift, causes a large change in its output. Missing more lesions in one patient group is a reason to investigate; it does not by itself show this kind of sensitivity to small changes.

### Why disease prevalence matters

**Positive predictive value** asks: among the slides the detector calls positive, how many are truly positive? False positive calls matter to that calculation. When disease is uncommon, the many negative slides can supply enough false positives to make a positive call less reliable.

The sliders let you try this calculation with 10,000 classifications. The prevalence slider changes the proportion that are truly positive. The separation slider changes how easily the model distinguishes the positive and negative groups.

For this calculation, sensitivity and specificity both equal 1 / (1 + exp(-separation)), with a fixed cutoff. Specificity is the fraction of truly negative slides called negative. Moving prevalence alone leaves both rates unchanged; increasing separation improves both.

The counts may have decimal places because they are calculated averages. This calculation is separate from the observed slide counts above; it cannot predict how the laboratory's detector will perform.
