# Model Under Pressure

> **Synthetic case:** A vendor-validated metastasis detector loses sensitivity after transfer to a laboratory using a different scanner and staining workflow.

## Your task

Distinguish supervised learning, convolutional image models, and generative AI. Diagnose generalizability, bias, brittleness, and drift precisely, then define local verification and monitoring with human oversight.

## Key point

AI performance belongs to a model **in a defined population and workflow**. Aggregate metrics may conceal failure in clinically important subgroups.
