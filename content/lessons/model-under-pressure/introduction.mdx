# Model Under Pressure

You are the pathology resident assisting Dr. Rao with a proposed metastasis second-review tool. The vendor reports detection of 192 of 200 metastasis-positive slides (96%) on its supported scanner. Your laboratory uses two scanners and a different staining workflow. Local review finds 42 of 50 positive slides (84%), including 17 of 25 micrometastases (68%) and 25 of 25 macrometastases (100%).

Before discussing release, the committee also wants to distinguish this tool from three other proposals: an explicit laboratory rule, gene-expression clustering and a generated tumor-board summary. Your task is to identify what each does and what evidence it needs.

## What you will be able to do

- Classify four proposals and choose the first review each needs.
- Inspect performance by scanner and lesion size.
- Distinguish initial generalizability failure from a later performance change.
- Explain how prevalence affects predictive value and assign oversight questions.

## Your task

Complete the three parts, then check the review. The sliders explore a mathematical example; the observed vendor, local and monthly results remain fixed.
