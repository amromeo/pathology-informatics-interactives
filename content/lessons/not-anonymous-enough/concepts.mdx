### Work through the first filter combination

In the [proposed export](#raw-privacy-table), select **Breast carcinoma**. Three of the 18 rows remain. Add **02108** as the five-digit ZIP and only **NR-001** is left. Each filter you add means a row has to match every fact you have chosen. A group of three is small in this file; that does not mean breast carcinoma is a rare disease.

Suppose someone already knows a person's diagnosis and ZIP. Those two facts could link that person to this row. Narrowing the file to one row shows that a link is possible; it does not prove that anyone outside has actually identified the right person. The key, sitting in the shared folder, is a second and much more direct way to link codes back to people.

Try the combination yourself, then change or clear a filter. The count always uses the same 18 records. To confirm an isolated record, you need at least two filters and exactly one matching row.
