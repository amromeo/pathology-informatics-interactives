## Debrief

**Informatics principle:** Deidentification is a governed risk-management process, not a synonym for removing names.

The original dataset remains coded and reidentifiable because a rare diagnosis combined with a five-digit ZIP isolates one synthetic record and the linkage key is inadequately protected. If you used the workbench, you also saw that transforming one field at a time leaves other distinguishing combinations. Uncontrolled release risks unauthorized disclosure of protected health information.

Increasing the smallest displayed group size reduced uniqueness in this teaching table, but the counter did not approve a release. Safe Harbor or Expert Determination review, or a properly governed limited-data-set pathway, still requires documented institutional review.

The data steward and privacy or research offices define safeguards and agreements. The clinical data owner approves the permitted use and minimum necessary release.
