### What happened in this case


Jordan removed names and MRNs but kept a lookup key in the shared folder. That key can reconnect an exported code to the patient. The [original export](#raw-privacy-table) also lets a person combine diagnosis and ZIP to distinguish NR-001 from the other 17 rows.

The original dataset is coded and reidentifiable. Filtering to one row demonstrates a possible linkage using outside knowledge; it does not show that a real person has been identified. Breast carcinoma appears in three rows of this selected file. Its frequency in this file says nothing about its frequency in the hospital population.

After broadening ZIP and diagnosis, the groups contain four, four, five and five rows. The [transformed preview](#transformed-privacy-preview) shows why the minimum is four. Changing the common admission date to year leaves that count unchanged. Protecting the separate key also leaves the count unchanged. Each action addresses a different part of the proposed disclosure.

The complete proposal passes five exercise checks only after the field changes, key restriction, purpose, recipient and agreement are recorded. Priya still must review the whole export, the known April 18 cohort, the ZIP population conditions and the codes. If exact timing or geography is needed for the study, she can consider a limited-data-set pathway that retains permissible detail. A larger group count alone cannot choose or approve the method.

Jordan prepares the approved fields and protects the key. The investigator explains which details are needed to study completion and calls. Priya and the research office determine the required disclosure review and agreement with the responsible data owner.
