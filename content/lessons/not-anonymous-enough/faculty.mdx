# Faculty guide: Not Anonymous Enough

Ask learners to explain how the combination of fields—not just one field—changes reidentification risk. Removing the linkage key is a real safeguard, but it does not make detailed dates, geography, and a rare cohort suitable for public release.

Make the regulatory distinction explicit: the HIPAA Privacy Rule recognizes Safe Harbor and Expert Determination as deidentification methods. A limited data set remains protected health information and requires an appropriate purpose, recipient, and data use agreement. Local policies and legal review remain authoritative; this exercise is educational and not legal advice.
