# Review a proposed field list with the privacy team

## Who to meet

A research data steward or privacy officer and, if possible, the analyst who prepares laboratory exports.

## Time

30–60 minutes. Bring the local policy on releasing data, and an approved request or a blank request form.

## Steps

1. Write down the research question, who would receive the data and which fields are requested. If there is no local request to use, use this lesson's file.
2. Ask the analyst how the records are chosen, which system each field comes from and whether a separate key exists.
3. For each field, note why the investigator needs it and what is lost if it is removed or made broader.
4. Find out which way of sharing has been approved and who approves it. Ask whether dates or ZIP codes are needed, and how that changes the method.
5. Find out who may use the key, where that access is approved and how the final file is checked against the approved field list.

## Bring back

A field list with four columns: the detail requested, why it is needed, the proposed change and the review it needs. Add the roles responsible for the key and for approving the release.

## Discuss with faculty

Would removing the date column hide the date if the project title names the day of admission? What other fields would the investigator need to study test completion and phoned critical results?

## Privacy

Use field names and policies for your notes. Keep patient rows, study codes and keys in the hospital's own systems. Do not export a local file as part of this exercise.
