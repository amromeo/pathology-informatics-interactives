# Local practicum

With a supervisor, obtain your institution's policy for classifying and releasing clinical data for research, quality improvement, or education.

1. Classify the synthetic dataset described in this lesson under the local policy.
2. Identify which retained fields—such as exact dates, five-digit ZIP codes, rare diagnoses, or a linkage code—change the classification or required safeguards.
3. Determine who decides whether the requested fields are necessary for the stated question.
4. Identify who may approve a release and which agreement or documented review is required for the intended recipient.
5. Record where a linkage key would be stored and which role would be permitted to use it.

Use only the lesson's synthetic example. Do not copy local patient data, linkage keys, or restricted policy details into teaching notes.
