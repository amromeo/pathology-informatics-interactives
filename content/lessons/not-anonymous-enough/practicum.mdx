# Review a proposed field list with the privacy team

## Who to meet

Meet a research data steward or privacy officer and, if available, the analyst who prepares laboratory exports.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with the local data-release policy and an approved request or blank request form.

## Steps

1. State the research question, proposed recipient and fields requested. Use this lesson's file if a local request is unavailable.
2. Ask the analyst to explain how records are selected, which system supplies each field and whether a separate key exists.
3. For each field, record why the investigator needs it and what is lost if it is removed or broadened.
4. Find the documented disclosure method and who approves it. Ask whether dates or ZIP codes are needed and how that affects the proposed method.
5. Identify who may use the key, where access is approved and how the final file is checked against the approved field list.

## Bring back

Prepare a field list with four columns: requested detail, reason needed, proposed change and required review. Add the roles responsible for the key and release approval.

## Discuss with faculty

Would removing a date column hide the date if the project title names the day of admission? What additional fields would the investigator need to study test completion and critical calls?

## Privacy

Use field names and policies for your notes. Keep patient rows, linkage codes and keys within approved hospital systems. Do not export a local file as part of this observation exercise.
