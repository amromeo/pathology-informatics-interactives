# Not Anonymous Enough

> **Synthetic case:** A research export contains no names, but retains exact dates, full ZIP codes, rare diagnoses, and a broadly accessible linkage key.

## Your task

Inspect which identifiers and distinguishing details remain, classify the export as presented, and choose a release path. Then run that path against field-minimization, linkage-key, uniqueness-risk, recipient, and confidentiality checks.

## Key point

Removing direct identifiers does not guarantee privacy. Coded protected health information, a limited data set, and data reviewed under a HIPAA deidentification method have different properties, obligations, and residual risks.
