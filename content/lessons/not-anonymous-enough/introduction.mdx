# Not Anonymous Enough

> **Synthetic case:** A research export contains no names, but retains exact dates, full ZIP codes, rare diagnoses, and a broadly accessible linkage key.

## Your task

Classify the dataset correctly, test how records could be singled out, and design a release path appropriate to the recipient and intended use.

## Concept lens

Removing direct identifiers does not guarantee privacy. Public, deidentified, and coded data have different properties, obligations, and residual risks.
