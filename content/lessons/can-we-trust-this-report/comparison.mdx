### Why the mean and median disagree

The **mean** is calculated by adding the TAT for every case and dividing by the number of cases. A small number of very long cases can pull the mean upward.

The **median** is the TAT of the middle case after the cases are arranged from shortest to longest. A few very long cases have less effect on the median.

In this report, the post-go-live median is slightly lower than the pre-go-live median, but the post-go-live mean is higher. That usually means the post-go-live cases are **skewed**: most cases are clustered toward the shorter end, with a smaller group of much longer cases forming a long tail.

The report does not yet tell you when those long cases occurred. Before deciding that TAT improved or worsened, look at the post-go-live period week by week.
