# Check how a local report is made

## Who to meet

The quality coordinator and the LIS analyst who builds reports. Choose a report and source records you are allowed to review.

## Time

30–60 minutes.

## Steps

Write down the question the report answers, which kinds of case it includes, its date range, and the start and end times it uses. Ask the analyst to show you how the query picks records. Find out what happens to a case with a missing time, a later amendment, or a final report that comes after the usual reporting date.

Follow one case from the original record, through the export, to the summary. Repeat its calculation and note any step someone does by hand. Compare the number of eligible accessions with the number in the summary. If they differ, find out which cases were left out and why.

If the report covers downtime, a system change or another unusual stretch, look at that stretch on its own, but keep its cases in the report. Ask the director whether the report supports the conclusion people plan to draw from it.

## Bring back

One worked calculation, with notes, and a short account of which cases were included, which were left out, and any you could not account for. Name the person who can answer each open question.

## Discuss with faculty

Which cases could be missing from the summary, and how would leaving them out change what it seems to show?

## Privacy

Keep patient identifiers and screenshots of source records in the hospital's own systems.
