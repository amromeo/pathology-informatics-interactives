# Check how a local report is made

## Who to meet

Meet the quality coordinator and LIS report analyst. Select a report and source records you are authorized to review.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes.

## Steps

Write down the report's question, eligible case types, date range, start event and endpoint. Ask the analyst to show how the query selects records. Find out what happens to a case with a missing time, a later amendment or a final report after the usual reporting date.

Trace one case from the original record through the export to the summary. Reproduce its calculation and identify any manual step. Compare the number of eligible accessions with the number included in the summary; account for exclusions rather than assuming the counts should match.

If the report spans downtime, a system change or another unusual interval, inspect that interval separately while keeping its cases visible. Ask the director whether the report supports the proposed conclusion.

## Bring back

Bring back one annotated calculation and a short account of the included, excluded and unresolved cases. Name the person who can answer each remaining question.

## Discuss with faculty

Which cases could be missing from the summary, and how would their omission change its conclusion?

## Privacy

Keep patient identifiers and source screenshots within approved institutional systems.
