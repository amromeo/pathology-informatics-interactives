# Apply it at your institution

## Who to meet

Meet a quality lead and report analyst.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Reconcile the export count with the accession list.
2. With your supervisor, select one approved record or workflow to examine.
3. Reconcile a small approved turnaround extract against its source and list missing fields and exclusions.
4. Compare the proposed change with local policy and identify who would test it and who would approve it.

## Bring back

Bring a one-page annotated diagram, table or checklist that documents this task: reconcile a small approved turnaround extract against its source and list missing fields and exclusions. Include one unresolved question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

What observation would change your conclusion? Explain why the shortcut “remove records with unexpectedly long times” could mislead the team.

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a workflow description without patient identifiers.

## Further local discussion

#### Local practicum

With a supervisor, choose one local laboratory quality report or dashboard.

1. Write down the exact measure, eligible cases, start point, endpoint, reporting period, and exclusions.
2. Trace one field from the LIS to the final spreadsheet or dashboard. Note any manual step, query, or filter.
3. Check whether a downtime, cutover, method change, staffing change, or other unusual interval is mixed into a longer comparison period. If so, review that interval separately without deleting its cases.
4. Identify who prepares the report and who in the laboratory approves its clinical or operational use.
5. Ask what check prevents an amended report or repeated export from counting the same accession twice.

Do not copy patient identifiers into the worksheet.
