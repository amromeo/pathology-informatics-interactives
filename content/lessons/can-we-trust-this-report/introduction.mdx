# Can We Trust This Report?

## Check the spreadsheet

The hospital recently replaced its laboratory information system (LIS). The new LIS went live on Monday, February 16. You are a pathology resident working with the director of surgical pathology. The laboratory wants to know whether surgical pathology turnaround time (TAT) changed after go-live.

The quality coordinator has prepared a spreadsheet that compares two periods:

- **Pre-go-live:** January 5–February 13, the six weeks before the new LIS went live.
- **Post-go-live:** February 16–April 3, the cutover week and the following six weeks.

The director asks you to review the comparison before it is presented at the monthly quality meeting.

### How the report was prepared

1. The quality coordinator asked the LIS analyst for a list of all biopsies and resections accessioned during the pre- and post-go-live periods.
2. The LIS analyst used the reporting function in the LIS to make that list. The analyst then saved the list as a spreadsheet, with one case on each row. This is often called an **export** because information is copied out of the LIS into another file.
3. The spreadsheet contains 210 pre-go-live cases and 245 post-go-live cases. Every case has a TAT value.
4. The Summary tab shows the number of cases, mean TAT, and median TAT for each period.

For this report, the department defines TAT as working days from accession to the **initial final report**. Biopsies and resections that meet the department's usual eligibility rules are included. Later amendments are tracked separately and must not add a second row for the same accession.

## Terms used in this case

### How data are recorded

- **Field:** one defined place for one item. *Example: one field holds the specimen collection time.*
- **Record:** the related fields kept for one case or accession. *Example: a surgical pathology accession record includes the accession number, specimen type, collection time, and case status.*
- **Structured data:** data stored in defined fields and formats. *Example: “colon biopsy” is selected from a list and stored in the specimen-type field.*
- **Unstructured data:** data recorded outside those fields. *Example: a handwritten collection time appears on a scanned requisition.*
- **Query or filter:** instructions that select which records and fields to include. *Example: select all colon biopsies finalized last month.*
- **Spreadsheet export:** a copy of selected LIS data placed in rows and columns. *Example: each row is an accession, with columns for accession time, final-report time, and specimen type.*
