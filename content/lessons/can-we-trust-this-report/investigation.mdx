## What happened during cutover?

When the quality coordinator looked at the cases week by week, cutover week stood out.

- The first eight cases were accessioned in the new LIS on Monday and early Tuesday.
- At 08:30 on Tuesday, accessioning in the new LIS was switched off while the LIS team fixed a go-live problem. It stayed off until Friday afternoon.
- The other 27 cases were recorded on their requisitions and in a paper cutover log. When they were entered into the LIS later, the original accession times were not put into the accession-time field.
- The coordinator found those times on the requisitions and in the cutover log, so TAT could still be calculated in the spreadsheet.
- Surgical pathology fell behind. Several cutover cases took more than ten working days to reach the initial final report.

All 35 cutover cases were counted in the original post-go-live group. Next, look at one of the affected cases and check where its accession time was recorded.

The accessioning supervisor told the coordinator that during cutover, cases were accessioned on paper as soon as they arrived. So the RECEIVED stamp on the requisition and the time in the cutover log record the same moment.
