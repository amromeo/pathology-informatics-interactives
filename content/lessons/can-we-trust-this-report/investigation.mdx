## What happened during cutover?

When the quality coordinator reviewed the cases by week, the cutover week stood out.

- The first eight cases were accessioned directly in the new LIS on Monday and early Tuesday.
- At 8:30 Tuesday morning, the accessioning function was taken offline while the LIS team corrected a go-live problem.
- The remaining 27 cases were recorded on their requisitions and in a paper cutover log. When those cases were entered into the LIS later, the original accession times were not entered into the structured accession-time field.
- The quality coordinator recovered the accession times from the requisitions and cutover log so that TAT could still be calculated for those cases in the spreadsheet.
- Surgical pathology developed a backlog. Several cutover cases took more than ten working days to reach the initial final report.

All 35 cutover cases were included in the original post-go-live group. The next step is to inspect one of the affected cases and confirm where its accession time was recorded.
