## Other ways pathology uses data

This TAT report is a small data-science problem: decide the question, find the right cases and fields, check the data, analyze them, and work out what the result means. The same steps apply to the other uses of laboratory data below, which later lessons cover.

- **Statistics:** compare a new reagent lot with the current one, and decide whether the difference matters for patients.
- **Linked clinical data:** bring pathology reports, images, molecular results and the relevant parts of the EHR together for tumor board, without mixing up patients or specimens.
- **Image analysis:** measure features in a whole-slide image while keeping it tied to the right slide, block, specimen and case.
- **Artificial intelligence and machine learning:** check that a tool works on the laboratory's own material, and keeps working once it is in use.
