## Other ways pathology uses data

This TAT report is a small data-science problem: define the question, find the right cases and fields, check the data, analyze them, and decide what the result means. The same habits carry into later Topic 2 lessons.

- **Statistical analysis:** compare a new reagent lot with the current lot and decide whether a measured difference matters clinically.
- **Linked clinical data:** bring pathology reports, images, molecular findings, and relevant EHR information together for tumor board without mixing patients or specimens.
- **Image analysis:** measure features in a whole-slide image while preserving the link to the correct slide, block, specimen, and case.
- **Artificial intelligence and machine learning:** verify that a tool works on the laboratory's own material and continues to work after it is placed in service.

These are examples of work in pathology data science described in [PIER Essentials R5](https://www.apcprods.org/assets/docs/pier/R5/PIER_Essentials_R5.pdf) and the [API introductory teaching slides](https://www.pathologyinformatics.org/teaching-slide-sets).
