# Is the Shift Real?

> **Synthetic case:** Clinicians report higher results after a reagent-lot change. The mean increased, while the median barely moved and four extreme values dominate the tail.

## Your task

Choose summaries and an inferential approach that match the distribution, study design, and clinical question. Separate evidence of a population shift from the importance of individual extreme results.

## Key point

Means are sensitive to extreme values. Confidence intervals describe precision around an estimate. Statistical significance and clinical importance are related questions, not synonyms.
