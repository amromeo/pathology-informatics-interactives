# The Alert Everyone Ignores

> **Synthetic case:** A laboratory-order alert interrupts clinicians more than twelve thousand times per month. Acceptance is below 5%, varies by role, and no outcome is measured.

## Your task

Diagnose alert fatigue, targeting failure, automation effects, and governance gaps. Redesign the alert and choose process, outcome, burden, and balancing measures suitable for longitudinal or randomized evaluation.

## Concept lens

Acceptance is informative but not sufficient. A CDS tool can be accepted inappropriately, overridden appropriately, or improve a process without improving patient outcomes.
