# Apply it at your institution

## Who to meet

Meet a quality lead and clinical informatics analyst.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Define eligible decisions and balancing measures.
2. With your supervisor, select one approved record or workflow to examine.
3. Draft a pilot evaluation table with pre/post groups, denominators and safety measures.
4. Compare the proposed change with local policy and identify who would test it and who would approve it.

## Bring back

Bring a one-page annotated diagram, table or checklist that documents this task: draft a pilot evaluation table with pre/post groups, denominators and safety measures. Include one unresolved question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

What observation would change your conclusion? Explain why the shortcut “count dismissed alerts as prevented repeats” could mislead the team.

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a workflow description without patient identifiers.

## Further local discussion

#### Local practicum: prepare a CDS evaluation

Choose one existing laboratory-related intervention with faculty. Obtain its specification and a permitted aggregate message report. Identify whether each row is a presentation, user response, order decision, patient, or encounter.

Describe how a message would be linked to an actual clinical action. Propose a sample of accepted and overridden suggestions for clinical review; examining only acceptance misses appropriate overrides and harmful agreement.

Define the evaluation population, periods, comparison group if feasible, and denominators before calculating change. Include clinical appropriateness, staff burden, and delayed indicated testing. State which differences between periods or groups could confound the comparison.

Prepare a one-page recommendation identifying the clinical reviewer, technical maintainer, review schedule, and conditions for pausing, revising, or retiring the intervention. Review the proposal with faculty before requesting a local change.
