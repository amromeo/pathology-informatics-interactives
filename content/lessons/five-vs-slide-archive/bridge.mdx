### Test the workload the service will actually run

The annual volume averages about 60 megabytes per second over the entire year. That average is much lower than the 500 megabytes per second overnight peak. Test writing, clinical retrieval and competing requests together. State what was measured: time to the first field of view, sustained write throughput, failed requests and storage growth.

Research analysis should use a governed copy with documented versions and access. It should not consume the resources needed for current clinical reporting. Hold records whose identity is unresolved and preserve the source and correction history. A fast but incorrectly linked tumor-board view is not ready for use.
