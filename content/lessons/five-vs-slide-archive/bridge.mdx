### Test the load the archive will actually carry

Spread over the whole year, 1.9 PB works out to about 60 megabytes (MB) a second. The overnight peak is 500 MB a second, more than eight times as much. Test scanning, clinical viewing and other requests at the same time. Write down what you measured: time to the first field of view, how fast data can be written over a long run, failed requests and storage growth.

Researchers should work from a separate copy, with its version and who can open it written down, so their analyses do not slow the system clinicians use for reporting. Hold any record whose identity is still in doubt, and keep its source and the history of every correction. A tumor-board view that is fast but linked to the wrong specimen is not ready to use.
