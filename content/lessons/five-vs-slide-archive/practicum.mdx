# Trace one finding into a tumor-board view

## Who to meet

A supervising pathologist and the analyst who looks after a combined clinical view.

## Time

30–60 minutes.

## Steps

1. Choose one approved example with your supervisor.
2. Find the patient, case, specimen, block and image identifiers.
3. Trace one molecular or protein finding back to its signed report.
4. Find out which clinical encounter the stage came from.
5. Ask how links that cannot be confirmed are shown and corrected.

## Bring back

A one-page diagram of the identifiers, the source records and the team responsible for each.

## Discuss with faculty

Which link needs someone else to confirm it, and what would stop a finding from the wrong specimen appearing in the view?

## Privacy

Use the hospital's approved learning environment, and keep patient identifiers off the diagram you take away.
