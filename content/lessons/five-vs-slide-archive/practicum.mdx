## Local practicum: trace a finding into a tumor-board view

### Who to meet

A supervising pathologist and the analyst responsible for a combined clinical view.

### Time

30–60 minutes.

### Steps

1. Select one approved example with your supervisor.
2. Identify the patient, case, specimen, block and image identifiers.
3. Trace one molecular or protein finding to its signed source report.
4. Identify the clinical episode used for staging.
5. Ask how unresolved links are displayed and corrected.

### Bring back

A one-page diagram of identifiers, source records and responsible teams.

### Discuss with faculty

Which link needs independent confirmation, and what would prevent a wrong-specimen finding from appearing?

### Privacy

Use the institution's approved learning environment and keep patient identifiers out of the take-home diagram.
