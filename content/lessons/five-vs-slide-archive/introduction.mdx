# Five Vs in the Slide Archive

> **Synthetic case:** A digital slide archive meets its nominal storage target but cannot sustain peak scanning while serving clinical retrieval and research analytics.

## Your task

Use volume, velocity, variety, veracity, and value to design a data flow that distinguishes low-latency clinical work from governed analytic use.

## Concept lens

“Big data” is not simply a large file count. Different workloads require different architectures, and images without reliable patient, case, and provenance linkage have limited clinical or analytic value.
