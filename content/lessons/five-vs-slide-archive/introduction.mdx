# Five Vs in the Slide Archive

You are the pathology resident helping Dr. Rao prepare the lung cancer tumor board. A new combined view brings together whole-slide images, a PD-L1 protein staining result, a genomic report and staging from the electronic health record (EHR). During review, the team finds that some records belong to the right patient but the wrong specimen or clinical episode.

The laboratory information system (LIS) records the case, part and block. The slide archive stores image files and image identifiers. A separate genomic laboratory sends reports. The clinical team records stage in the EHR. The combined view must preserve these relationships before the board uses it.

## What you will be able to do

- Apply volume, velocity, variety, veracity and value to this archive.
- Distinguish patient, specimen, block and encounter links.
- Repair mismatched links while retaining a correctly linked result.
- Choose a capacity test that includes peak scanning and clinical retrieval.

## Your task

Review the five archive observations, inspect each source record, and choose the action needed. Then decide how to test the combined workload. You may open the debrief at any time.
