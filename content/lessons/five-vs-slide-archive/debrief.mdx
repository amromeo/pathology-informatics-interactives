## Debrief

**Informatics principle:** The five Vs are independent design constraints.

The initial architecture modeled storage capacity but not burst ingestion, metadata variation, linkage quality, or workload separation. Scanner backlogs and slow clinical retrieval were therefore predictable even though total disk capacity appeared sufficient.

Infrastructure and digital pathology teams own implementation. Pathology leadership approves diagnostic service levels, image fidelity, and intended analytic use.
