# The Missing CBC

> **Synthetic case:** The emergency department is treating a patient with a suspected gastrointestinal bleed. The CBC is still listed as pending in the EHR, and the team is waiting for the hemoglobin.

The ED calls the hematology lab to ask whether the specimen has been tested. The clinician does not know whether the CBC is complete; the only information available in the EHR is **Pending**.

## Your task

Identify the hardware, software, network connections, and interfaces involved in electronic result reporting. Then use the records from each system to find where the report stopped.

This lesson follows the CBC from the analyzer to a vendor-hosted EHR. All patients, results, screens, and logs are synthetic educational artifacts.
