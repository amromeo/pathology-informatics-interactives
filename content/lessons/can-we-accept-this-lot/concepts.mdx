## Describe what happened in these specimens

The **mean** is the average difference across all specimens. The **median** is the middle difference after the specimens are arranged from lowest to highest. Comparing them helps show whether a few larger differences are affecting the average.

The **standard deviation** describes how widely the individual differences are spread around their mean. A small standard deviation means the two lots differ by a similar amount across specimens. A larger standard deviation means the agreement varies more from specimen to specimen.

In this comparison, several larger positive differences create a modest tail on the right side of the dot plot. There is no single, markedly discordant specimen. The tail makes the mean somewhat higher than the median.
