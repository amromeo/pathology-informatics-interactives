## Debrief: What supports accepting this lot?

The [paired-results table](#lot-results-table) contains 30 specimens measured with both lots. The main numerical comparison uses the 18 near the laboratory's clinical decision concentration.

Their differences sum to +21.6 ng/L. Dividing by 18 gives a mean of +1.2 ng/L. The median is +0.8 ng/L and the SD is about 1.2 ng/L. The larger positive differences explain why the mean exceeds the median.

The confidence interval is approximately +0.6 to +1.8 ng/L. The paired t-test gives p < 0.001, providing evidence against a zero average difference. That answers whether a difference can be detected. The laboratory must also ask whether its size is acceptable.

This case's director had already specified an acceptable average shift from −2 to +2 ng/L. The entire confidence interval lies inside that range. The six very low specimens also retain the same less-than-2 ng/L reportable category, and all six elevated specimens meet the separate ±10% criterion.

Together, those findings support acceptance after director review. The chemistry team should document the positive shift and follow the planned monitoring after the lot change. Neither a small p-value alone nor a mean inside the limit establishes that all required checks passed.

## What should the learner carry forward?

Keep measurements from the same specimen paired. State which concentration group each summary describes. Distinguish the spread of individual differences from uncertainty in their mean. Choose the acceptance criteria before inspecting the results.

The ±2 ng/L and ±10% limits belong to this laboratory's case. They are not universal troponin limits or a complete implementation of a reagent-lot guideline. A local protocol must address the assay, concentration ranges, individual differences, sampling and risks relevant to its use.

PIER 2.2 connects these descriptive statistics, statistical inference and test choices to laboratory decisions. The statistical calculation informs the director's review; it does not replace it.
