## Start with one pair

Each row in the [paired-results table](#lot-results-table) contains two measurements from the same specimen. The worksheet subtracts the current-lot result from the new-lot result.

For TNI-013, the current lot gives 18.0 ng/L and the new lot gives 18.7 ng/L. The difference is 18.7 − 18.0 = **+0.7 ng/L**. The plus sign means the new lot reads higher. TNI-012 goes in the other direction: 16.7 − 17.5 = **−0.8 ng/L**.

The table initially shows the 18 specimens near the clinical decision concentration. The dot plot below shows their differences. A dot at zero means the two lots gave the same numerical result. Dots to the right mean higher new-lot results; dots to the left mean lower results.

Inspect the pattern before choosing an answer. Are most differences on one side of zero? How far apart are they? Is one specimen separated from all the others? You can change the table's range filter to inspect the six very low or six elevated specimens; the main statistical comparison continues to use the same 18 specimens.
