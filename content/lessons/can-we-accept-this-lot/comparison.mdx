## Start with the individual results

The worksheet calculates each difference as **new lot minus current lot**. For example, a current-lot result of 18 ng/L and a new-lot result of 19 ng/L produce a difference of +1 ng/L.

A positive difference means the new lot gave a higher result. A negative difference means it gave a lower result.

First inspect the 18 specimens near the clinical decision concentration. Use the table and dot plot to answer three questions:

1. Does the new lot usually read higher or lower?
2. Are the differences tightly grouped or widely spread?
3. Does one specimen stand apart from all the others?

Do this before using the summary statistics. A mean, median, or standard deviation is easier to interpret after you have seen the results it summarizes.
