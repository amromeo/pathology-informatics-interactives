# Review a local lot-comparison protocol

## Who to meet

Meet the chemistry director or supervisor. Ask to review an approved protocol and a completed study you are authorized to inspect.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes.

## Steps

Identify the patient specimens, concentration groups and paired results. Work through one difference. Find the acceptance limits in the protocol and establish whether they were specified before the results were reviewed.

Ask which checks concern individual specimens, which concern an average shift and which compare reportable categories. If the report includes a confidence interval or p-value, explain what question it answers. Identify any required checks that those statistics do not replace.

## Bring back

Bring back a one-page explanation of the decision: the result compared with each criterion, who approved the lot and what was monitored afterward. Note one feature that differs from this lesson's protocol.

## Discuss with faculty

What finding would prevent approval even if the mean shift met the limit?

## Privacy

Keep identifiers and source records within approved institutional systems.
