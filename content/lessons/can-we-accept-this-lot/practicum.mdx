# Review a local lot-comparison protocol

## Who to meet

The chemistry director or supervisor. Ask to see an approved protocol and a completed lot comparison you are allowed to review.

## Time

30–60 minutes.

## Steps

Find the patient specimens, the concentration groups and the paired results. Work through one difference yourself. Find the acceptance limits in the protocol and check that they were written down before anyone looked at the results.

Ask which checks look at individual specimens, which look at the average shift and which compare reporting categories. If the report includes a confidence interval or a p-value, explain what question each one answers. Note any required checks that those statistics cannot replace.

## Bring back

A one-page account of the decision: how the results compared with each criterion, who approved the lot and what was monitored afterward. Note one way the local protocol differs from the one in this lesson.

## Discuss with faculty

What finding would stop approval even if the mean shift met the limit?

## Privacy

Keep patient identifiers and source records in the hospital's own systems.
