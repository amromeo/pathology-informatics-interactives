## What can we conclude beyond these specimens?

The 18 specimens give the laboratory an estimate of the average lot difference, but another set of specimens would not produce exactly the same mean. Inferential statistics describe that uncertainty.

The **95% confidence interval** gives the range of average lot differences reasonably supported by this comparison. It does not contain 95% of the individual specimen differences. It describes uncertainty around the estimated mean difference.

The **p-value** addresses a narrower question: do these specimens provide evidence of an average difference between the lots? It does not tell us whether the difference is large enough to affect patient care.

This laboratory decided before testing that an average shift between −2 and +2 ng/L near the clinical decision concentration would be medically acceptable. The confidence interval must be compared with that limit. The p-value alone cannot make the lot-acceptance decision.
