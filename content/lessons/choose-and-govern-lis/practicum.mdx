## Local practicum: write requirements that can be checked

With faculty, choose an LIS module or planned upgrade. Write three mandatory requirements and three preferences, each connected to a named laboratory workflow. Add a test script and expected result for every mandatory item.

Review a permitted proposal or internal project plan. Separate observed evidence, supplier statements, demonstrated failures, and unanswered questions. Compare the same cost categories over a defined period, identifying allowances and exclusions.

Choose one dictionary or order-set maintenance issue, such as an obsolete immunohistochemistry panel. Propose a controlled review of its users, names, components, procedure, downstream reports, approval, testing, and retirement. Do not change production settings as part of this exercise.

Present a short implementation and lifecycle plan identifying local acceptance tests, staff training, support, change control, downtime/reconciliation, historical access, and responsibilities at termination.
