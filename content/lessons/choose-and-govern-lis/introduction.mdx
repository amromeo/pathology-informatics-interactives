# Selecting and Maintaining a Laboratory Information System

You are the pathology representative on the committee replacing the laboratory information system (LIS). The current contract is approaching renewal. Laboratory staff, IT, procurement, and clinical services have reviewed three vendors.

Alder received the highest preference ratings after the demonstrations. Before the committee recommends a contract, the laboratory director asks you to check the actual evidence for corrected reports, recovery, and historical record access. The initial comparison also omits several implementation and exit costs.

Your task is to separate required capabilities from preferences, classify what the demonstrations establish, compare a defined five-year cost scope, and plan responsibilities after installation. An attractive interface is useful, but it cannot compensate for a failed report-correction workflow.

Start with the procurement summary and approved requirements. The vendor records, costs, and recovery targets belong to this selection exercise; they are not market prices or universal laboratory requirements.
