# Choose and Govern the LIS

> **Synthetic project:** A vendor wins a polished demonstration, but only 61% of laboratory requirements were verified. Interface cost, data access, backup, and exit planning were omitted.

## Your task

Translate workflows into weighted requirements, use RFI and RFP stages appropriately, verify claims, and plan the full lifecycle: selection, installation, testing, training, go-live, maintenance, change control, recovery, and retirement.

## Concept lens

The best demonstration is not necessarily the best system. Transparent requirements make tradeoffs reviewable and keep the laboratory’s clinical obligations visible throughout procurement.
