### Turn a workflow into a testable requirement

A requirement should identify a user, a task, the needed behavior, and evidence that would demonstrate it. “Supports corrections” is too vague. “A corrected report becomes current in the EHR, the prior version remains available, and the communication workflow is triggered” can be tested.

The committee approved three mandatory requirements before ranking vendors. The recovery exercise uses a four-hour service recovery target and a maximum fifteen-minute interval of data requiring reconciliation. These local targets reflect the case's operational planning. In practice, define acceptable downtime and data loss with laboratory leadership, IT, and the clinical services that depend on the results.

A **request for information (RFI)** asks the market what capabilities and approaches are available. A **request for proposal (RFP)** asks shortlisted suppliers to respond to defined requirements, scope, responsibilities, pricing, and contract terms. A brochure response is not equivalent to an observed acceptance test.

### Distinguish failed evidence from missing evidence

Classify each record below. A demonstrated failure needs correction and retesting. An incomplete demonstration or unsupported assertion needs additional evidence. Both can prevent selection now, but they do not establish the same thing about the product.

The assessment should identify the product version and environment. A future patch is a proposal, not a tested capability. A standard vendor demonstration also cannot establish that the installed local interfaces and dictionaries will work.

### Keep mandatory requirements separate from preferences

Usability, analytics, and training quality can be compared with preference scores after necessary capabilities are addressed. Agree on the weights before interpreting a ranking. The weights describe priorities; they do not measure clinical safety.

Do not relabel a failed mandatory requirement as a preference merely to preserve a favored vendor's ranking. A legitimate requirement change needs a documented clinical and operational rationale, approval, and reassessment of all vendors against the revised scope.

The classification exercise provides feedback after you review your decisions. Even if a learner marks every row as passing, the recommendation cannot ignore the supplied evidence of failure or incomplete verification.
