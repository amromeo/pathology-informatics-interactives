## Debrief: a ranking does not replace evidence

Alder's attractive preference score did not resolve its failed correction demonstration or unverified export. Cedar's recovery exercise passed, but its export lacked required history and identifiers; its correction demonstration was incomplete. Birch had supporting evidence for all three mandatory requirements in the supplied records.

That makes Birch eligible for further contract and local acceptance review in this exercise. It does not certify the product or establish that it is best for every institution. Deferring a decision is also defensible when the team identifies the unanswered questions, assigns responsibility, and sets a date for review.

The initial five-year totals were 580, 565, and 510 thousand dollars for Alder, Birch, and Cedar. Including interfaces, migration, and exit changes them to 695, 695, and 720 thousand dollars. These scoped totals reveal omissions in the opening comparison; they do not constitute a complete budget or remove uncertainty in the allowance.

### Carry the clinical requirements through the lifecycle

Local installation testing must verify actual interfaces, configuration, users, and workflows. Training should include exceptions and downtime. A vendor-tested upgrade still needs an assessment of local effects and proportionate testing before approval.

During an outage, restoration and reconciliation are different tasks. After service returns, the laboratory must account for the orders and reports created while systems were unavailable. Before retirement, test historical retrieval, retention arrangements, and access permissions; a new system being live does not make the old records unnecessary.

Maintain procedures for general operation, LIS applications and dictionaries, interfaces, reports, quality checks, access, disaster recovery, and change control. Map evidence to applicable regulatory and accreditation requirements with the laboratory's quality team. A vendor contract or a passing demonstration does not establish compliance.
