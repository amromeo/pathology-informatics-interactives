### Preserve the cases that should behave differently

The opening encounter is only one test. Run the design against a collecting nurse, absent history, an older report, an unusable report, a pending order, a documented reason to repeat, and the exact 90-day boundary.

In this exercise, the rule uses collection time for a final report and includes exactly 90 days. A pending order must be described as pending, without implying that a final result exists. The documented exception retains access to the earlier information and the ability to proceed.

The checks assess the approved delivery specification. They do not certify clinical effectiveness. Both an inline suggestion and a soft stop may preserve the required decision, but their burden differs. Explain why an interruption is necessary before choosing one. Changing the design clears the previous test result, so rerun the encounters after a revision.
