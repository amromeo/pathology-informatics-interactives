# Five Rights for the Right Test

> **Synthetic case:** A long alert appears after a clinician signs a test order. It fires for every role and changes almost no orders.

## Your task

Map the decision workflow and redesign the intervention around the right person, information, channel, format, and point in workflow. Distinguish education, ordering, diagnostic, utilization, reflex, protocol, and alert-based support.

## Key point

Decision support should reduce cognitive work at the moment a decision is made. A correct policy delivered to the wrong person or at the wrong time is poor CDS.
