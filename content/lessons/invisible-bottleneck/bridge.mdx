### Test an operational change without removing clinical controls

First reconcile the two accessions still open at noon. Then pilot trained review
coverage around courier arrival while retaining the laboratory's review rules.
Repair and monitor the analytic refresh so the intervention can be evaluated
using sufficiently current data.

Define an outcome, a process measure, and balancing measures. An outcome is
receipt-to-release time, reported by arrival group with long delays and pending
ages. A process measure is time from analysis to review. Balancing measures
include incorrect releases, critical-result communication delays, unresolved
exceptions, overtime, and staffing cost. Improving one queue at the expense of
another bench is not an overall improvement.

Compare equivalent populations, schedules, and periods before and after the
pilot. Record staffing and arrival volume. The workbench identifies where to
investigate; it does not calculate a causal treatment effect or predict savings.
The final review requires a current linked dataset, examination of the
post-analysis interval, and a plan that preserves clinical safeguards.
