# Apply it at your institution

## Who to meet

Meet an operations manager and reporting analyst.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Check the extract inclusion rule.
2. With your supervisor, select one approved record or workflow to examine.
3. Write a dashboard definition with its population, timestamps, pending cases and an hourly summary.
4. Compare the proposed change with local policy and identify who would test it and who would approve it.

## Bring back

Bring a one-page annotated diagram, table or checklist that documents this task: write a dashboard definition with its population, timestamps, pending cases and an hourly summary. Include one unresolved question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

What observation would change your conclusion? Explain why the shortcut “sort the completed cases by longest time” could mislead the team.

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a workflow description without patient identifiers.
