# The Invisible Bottleneck

> **Synthetic case:** The LIS median looks acceptable, but middleware queue data reveal long peak delays while analyzers sit intermittently idle.

## Your task

Combine courier, analyzer, middleware, LIS, and business-intelligence data. Define a question, verify lineage and freshness, identify the bottleneck, and choose balanced clinical, operational, and financial measures.

## Key point

Ancillary data can reveal workflow behavior that a final-result table cannot. A metric must be timely and tied to a decision to support data-driven improvement.
