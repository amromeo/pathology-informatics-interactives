### What happened in this case


The original observation identifier and free-text answer could be stored but
were not usable by the agreed routing rules. The overall observation mapping
and coded answers restore the intended actions for NB-01, NB-02, and NB-03.

The other five tests protect different requirements: identity context, one
record for a retry, reconciliation of a corrected report, visible handling of
unknown answers, and preliminary-result review. The original configuration
matches five of eight actions; the complete repair matches eight of eight.
Removing the identity or version safeguards creates failures despite correct
terminology.

The laboratory and program agree on clinical meaning and follow-up. Interface
staff implement and monitor the exchange. Standards support that agreement;
they do not replace it or confirm that the receiving workflow is complete.

You selected the observation identifier and its allowed answers from a supplied
agreement. Next, investigate why laboratories need shared observation identifiers
at all, and verify candidate LOINC terms against a local catalog rather than
accepting a proposed code because it exists.

<summary>A closer look: ask what happens after the message arrives</summary>

A receiving program can store a message without placing it in the worklist used for follow-up. Staff from the laboratory and public-health program need to agree on the observation being reported, the allowed answers and the action associated with each answer.

Trace a corrected newborn-screening report through that agreement. The program needs to recognize which earlier observation it changes and whether follow-up must change. A retry of the same report also needs to remain distinguishable from a new observation.

Shared message formats and terminology help both teams describe these requirements. The acceptance test still needs to show what the program does with the report, including an unfamiliar answer and an incomplete patient match.

**At the joint review:** Which receiving worklist or follow-up record would you ask the program to show after each test message?
