# Newborn Screen to Public Health

> **Synthetic case:** A newborn-screening result message is accepted by the public-health receiver but cannot enter automated follow-up routing.

## Your task

Inspect the message profile, observation identifiers, answer concepts, units, facility identifiers, and receiving workflow. Compare the roles of HL7 v2, FHIR, terminology standards, implementation guides, and standards-development organizations.

## Concept lens

Transport and syntax can succeed while semantic and workflow interoperability fail. Standards must be profiled, implemented, and clinically governed for a defined use.
