# Apply it at your institution

## Who to meet

Meet a public-health interface lead.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Read the receiver implementation guide.
2. With your supervisor, select one approved record or workflow to examine.
3. Map a public-health report field to its message rule, terminology and receiving-team validation.
4. Compare the proposed change with local policy and identify who would test it and who would approve it.

## Bring back

Bring a one-page annotated diagram, table or checklist that documents this task: map a public-health report field to its message rule, terminology and receiving-team validation. Include one unresolved question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

What observation would change your conclusion? Explain why the shortcut “assume the latest standard needs no local testing” could mislead the team.

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a workflow description without patient identifiers.

## Further local discussion

#### Local practicum

Choose one reportable test. Trace its source, LIS, interface or health-information exchange, and public-health destination. Record the principal messages, identifiers, terminology, units, acknowledgments, and exception workflow.
