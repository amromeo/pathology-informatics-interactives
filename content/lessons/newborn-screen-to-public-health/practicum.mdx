# Trace one public health report at your institution

## Who to meet

The interface analyst who looks after a public health connection.

## Time

30–60 minutes.

## Steps

1. With your supervisor, choose one test the laboratory reports to public health.
2. Follow it from the instrument or LIS, through the interface or health information exchange, to the public health program.
3. Read the receiving program's implementation guide for that report.
4. For one result field, find the message rule, the test code and answer codes, and how the program checks what it receives.
5. Ask how acknowledgments, rejected messages and corrected reports are handled, and who would test and approve a change.

## Bring back

A one-page diagram of the path, with the main messages, identifiers, codes, units, acknowledgments and how exceptions are handled. Include one open question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

What would you need to see to believe the program understood the report? Why might assuming the newest standard needs no local testing mislead the team?

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor allows you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots in the hospital's own systems, and bring back a description with no patient identifiers.
