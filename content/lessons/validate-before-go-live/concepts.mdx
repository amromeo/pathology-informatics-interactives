## Define what is being validated

An **intended use** names the specimens, clinical purpose, users, acquisition system, viewer, display, and workflow. Routine fixed-tissue reporting, time-critical frozen consultation, and home reporting can have different risks and evidence needs. Include slide identity, complete tissue capture, image retrieval, navigation, reporting linkage, and access to glass when digital review is inadequate.

The 2021 CAP guideline update recommends at least 60 representative cases for one application, with 20 additional cases for relevant additional applications such as immunohistochemistry or special stains when not already represented. These are guideline recommendations, not evidence that any convenient set of 60 shows safety. Include the expected diagnostic spectrum, complexity, small findings, and difficult preparations; document any exclusions before reviewing outcomes.

**Intraobserver concordance** compares a pathologist's digital interpretation with that same person's glass interpretation. Comparing a new reader with the historical report mixes reader and modality differences. The guideline recommends at least two weeks between reads. Separate sessions, ordering of cases, access to prior interpretations, and training need planning to reduce recall and learning effects.

Record original interpretations before adjudication. Agree how to classify clinically important disagreements and what will trigger investigation. The guideline calls for investigating and attempting to remedy causes when concordance is below 95%. That is not permission to ignore a serious disagreement when the aggregate is higher.

For this laboratory, the director also requires documented resolution or an explicit hold for acquisition defects, review of every disagreement, complete intended-workflow checks, and signed scope approval. Those are the case's acceptance conditions, not a universal numerical release rule.

Concordance is assessed for each observer. A pooled percentage can hide one reader below the investigation threshold. Rao and Chen each agreed in 29 of their 30 paired cases (96.7%); V12 is Rao's discordance and V44 is Chen's.
