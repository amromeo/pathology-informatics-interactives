## Debrief

**Informatics principle:** Validation evidence must represent the scope of clinical deployment.

Focus failures clustered in frozen tissue, the review interval was short, and only one display site was tested. Full approval would extend beyond the evidence; permanent rejection would ignore a potentially remediable category-specific problem.

Digital pathology staff remediate acquisition and displays. Participating pathologists complete representative validation. The medical director approves the final scope and change-control conditions.
