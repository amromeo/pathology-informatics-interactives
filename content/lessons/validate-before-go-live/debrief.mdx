### What happened in this case


Fixed tissue has 58 agreements among 60 paired cases, or 96.7%. Frozen sections have 14/17, or 82.4%, with an inadequate interval for the guideline recommendation and too few cases for a completed application study. These denominators describe this packet, not the probability of a future patient error.

V12 requires acquisition review and corrective evidence. V44 requires clinical assessment of interpretive variation. Both stay in the original calculation. Agreement after adjudication is not the original intraobserver concordance.

A reasonable recommendation is to consider the hospital fixed-tissue scope after documented corrective review and signoff. Keep frozen consultation and home reporting on hold pending appropriate evidence. Do not mistake a satisfactory plan for completed corrective work. The exercise cannot grant approval.

Elena Park and the technical team investigate acquisition, linkage, retrieval, and versions. Participating pathologists assess interpretability, discordance significance, and training. The director approves the clinical scope with the relevant institutional teams. Ongoing quality review, a glass fallback, and change control remain necessary after launch.
