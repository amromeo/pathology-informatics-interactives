# Reviewing a Whole-Slide Imaging Validation

You are the resident on the digital pathology team. Dr. Anita Shah, director of surgical pathology, has been asked to approve fixed-tissue hematoxylin and eosin (H&E) reporting at the hospital, frozen-section consultation, and home reporting next month. Elena Park, the digital pathology technologist, assembled the study log after trained pathologists completed paired reads.

The packet contains a completed fixed-tissue comparison, an exploratory frozen-section series, two fixed-tissue discordance records, and a list of untested workflows. Decide what the evidence supports before the director signs. A favorable pooled percentage cannot settle a missing application, an unresolved acquisition problem, or an untested reporting location.

Start with the actual study counts. Build a defensible plan, investigate the discordances, choose the approval boundary, and classify later changes. The image demonstration in [Planning Image Storage and Delivery](../pixel-budget/) cannot replace this clinical evidence.


## What you will be able to do

- Review validation evidence for each intended use.
- Review intended use and local validation protocol.
- Explain why “reuse another institution validation unchanged” is an incomplete approach.

Start by reading the case records. Make a choice before checking the explanation; you can revise it and test again.
