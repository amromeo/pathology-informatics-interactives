# Apply it at your institution

## Who to meet

Meet the digital pathology director.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Review intended use and local validation protocol.
2. With your supervisor, select one approved record or workflow to examine.
3. Annotate a validation plan with intended use, readers, case mix, washout and discordance review.
4. Compare the proposed change with local policy and identify who would test it and who would approve it.

## Bring back

Bring a one-page annotated diagram, table or checklist that documents this task: annotate a validation plan with intended use, readers, case mix, washout and discordance review. Include one unresolved question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

What observation would change your conclusion? Explain why the shortcut “reuse another institution validation unchanged” could mislead the team.

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a workflow description without patient identifiers.

## Further local discussion

#### Local practicum

With the director and digital pathology lead, review an approved local plan. Identify the intended use, case-selection rationale, readers, read interval, original paired results, discordance investigation, exclusions, system versions, and signed approval. Compare the tested scope with actual reporting locations and preparation types.

Trace one subsequent system change to its risk assessment, verification evidence, training, and release approval. Ask how unreadable slides are handled and how glass review remains available. Report gaps to the responsible faculty; do not infer that this lesson's study design replaces local requirements.
