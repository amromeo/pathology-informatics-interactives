# Review one digital pathology validation at your institution

## Who to meet

The digital pathology director or lead, and the laboratory director.

## Time

30–60 minutes.

## Steps

1. With your supervisor, look at an approved local validation plan. Find the proposed use, why those cases were chosen, the readers, the gap between readings, the original paired results, how disagreements were investigated, any cases left out, the system versions and the signed approval.
2. Compare what was tested with where pathologists actually report and which kinds of preparation they read.
3. Follow one later change to the system through its risk review, the checks done, training and approval for release.
4. Ask how slides that cannot be read on screen are handled, and how pathologists get the glass when they need it.

## Bring back

A one-page summary of what the validation covers and what it does not. Include one open question and the person who can answer it. Report any gaps to the faculty responsible; do not assume this lesson's study design replaces local requirements.

## Discuss with faculty

What would you need to see before approving a new reporting location? Why is reusing another hospital's validation unchanged not enough?

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor allows you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots in the hospital's own systems, and bring back a description with no patient identifiers.
