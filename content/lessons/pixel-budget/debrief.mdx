## Debrief: savings need a defined clinical use

At 0.5 µm/pixel, one plane contains 600 million pixels and 1.8 GB before compression. At 0.25 µm/pixel it contains 2.4 billion pixels and 7.2 GB. Three planes triple those amounts. At a modeled 10:1 ratio and two copies, one year at 0.25 µm/pixel requires 180 TB for one plane. None of these calculations establishes an acceptable diagnostic setting.

Downsampling and strong compression each change displayed detail. Neither fixes the blurred source region. A lossless copy preserves the pixels it receives; it cannot reverse earlier loss. Cropping can preserve detail within its borders while omitting the rest of the specimen.
