# Choosing Image Settings for a Pathology Service

Dr. Rao, a surgical pathologist, asks you to review an archive proposal with Elena, the digital pathology technologist, and Malik from infrastructure. The service scans fixed-tissue hematoxylin and eosin (H&E) slides for hospital reporting. Malik proposes smaller files because the storage forecast exceeds the current allocation. Elena also reports locally blurred tissue in a few scans.

Your question is whether the proposed savings preserve information needed for the intended use. Compare the same tissue field after two different changes. Then estimate sampling, compression, focal-plane, and storage-copy tradeoffs. Recommend the evidence needed before changing the service's approved settings.

The comparison uses a small H&E-style field, not a whole-slide acquisition. It demonstrates visible raster-image changes; it cannot establish diagnostic equivalence, scanner resolution, a physical tissue scale, or an acceptable clinical compression ratio.
