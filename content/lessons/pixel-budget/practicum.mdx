# Local practicum

With digital pathology and infrastructure staff, trace one approved training slide from accession and barcode to scan, archive, retrieval, viewer, and reporting workstation. Record spacing, compression, focal planes, encoded size, time to first image, and the authoritative slide identity. Keep identifiers out of your notes.

Compare a month's actual volume and file sizes with this estimate. Add pyramids, rescans, copies, retention, growth, and recovery. Ask who approves a setting change and how restored images are checked for identity, completeness, and readability. Do not alter a clinical archive for this exercise.
