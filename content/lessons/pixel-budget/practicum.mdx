# Follow one slide through the image system at your institution

## Who to meet

A digital pathology technologist and someone from the infrastructure team.

## Time

30–60 minutes.

## Steps

1. With your supervisor, choose one approved training slide. Follow it from accession and barcode to scanning, the archive, retrieval, the viewer and the reporting workstation.
2. Note the pixel spacing, compression, number of focal planes, file size, time until the first image appears, and which system holds the official slide identity. Keep identifiers out of your notes.
3. Compare a month's actual scanning volume and file sizes with this lesson's estimate. Add image pyramids, rescans, copies, how long images are kept, growth and recovery.
4. Ask who approves a change to the settings, and how restored images are checked to make sure they are the right slide, complete and readable.

## Bring back

A one-page diagram of the slide's path with the measurements you found. Include one open question and the person who can answer it. Do not change anything in a clinical archive as part of this exercise.

## Discuss with faculty

What would you need to see before agreeing to a change in compression? Why might choosing the smallest file, whatever the detail lost, mislead the team?

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor allows you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots in the hospital's own systems, and bring back a description with no patient identifiers.
