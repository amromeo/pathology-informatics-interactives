# Faculty guide: The Steward at Morning Huddle

## Expected reasoning

Learners should resist choosing a single department to blame. The central move is to reconcile specimen-level source data with the patient- or service-level dashboard before changing operations.

## Watch for

- Confusing system availability with information quality.
- Treating a median as self-validating without inspecting exclusions.
- Assigning clinical approval to a technical analyst.

Use the case to ask which informatics activities the residents have already encountered in routine rotations.
