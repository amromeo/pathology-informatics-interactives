## Debrief

**Informatics principle:** A technically available dashboard can still be semantically unsafe for operational use.

The technical defect is the loss of structured received timestamps for manual accessions. The analytic consequence is denominator bias; delayed cases disappear before the median is calculated. The workflow consequence is false reassurance at the huddle, creating a risk that real delays remain uncorrected.

The LIS and analytics teams investigate and repair the data path. Laboratory operations confirms the workflow meaning. The medical director approves the metric’s clinical interpretation and use.
