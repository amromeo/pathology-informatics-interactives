## Debrief: what still needs to happen before Monday?

The assay should wait. The result reached the EHR, but part of the interpretive comment was cut off. Pathology has not reviewed the troponin alert. The section also needs to finish its procedures, training and staffing arrangements.

### Who finishes the work?

The LIS team builds the test and checks the interface. Hospital IT keeps the network and shared systems running. Pathology informatics helps chemistry check how the new assay fits the laboratory's ordering and reporting practices. The laboratory director approves the order and complete report.

The bench supervisor and laboratory operations staff prepare the section to run the test on every shift. Pathology and clinical informatics review the alert with the clinicians who will receive it. Sending a result successfully leaves all of those jobs to be checked.

### Follow the problem through the chart

The three calls ask different questions:

- **The corrected report is still wrong in the EHR.** The laboratory director should lead the review. The LIS and EHR teams can trace the correction and repair the display. The laboratory then checks the complete corrected report where clinicians read it.
- **Two troponin assays appear on one trend graph.** Pathology informatics should lead with chemistry and the EHR analyst. The laboratory has to decide whether the assays are comparable before their results are plotted together.
- **The alert fires again after a correction.** Clinical informatics should coordinate the alert review. Pathology informatics and the LIS team first check whether the correction replaced the original result or arrived as another result.

That last distinction matters. In a Health Level Seven version 2 (HL7 v2) message, status C identifies a corrected result on the same observation. If the laboratory sent a new result instead, the laboratory team needs to fix that first. If the correction arrived properly, the team can then examine why the EHR alert fired again.

### Check the report clinicians will use

The laboratory must check the complete report in its agreed location in the chart. A correct number in a flowsheet would not reveal the missing interpretive comment in this case. Ask the person reviewing the report to show the comment on screen before the director signs off.
