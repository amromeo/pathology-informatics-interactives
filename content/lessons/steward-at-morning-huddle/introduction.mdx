# Who Does What in Laboratory Informatics?

You are the chemistry pathology resident at Friday's meeting about a new high-sensitivity troponin assay. The laboratory plans to start patient testing on Monday.

> **Case:** The analyzer sent test results through the laboratory information system (LIS) to the electronic health record (EHR). The laboratory still needs to check the report and finish preparing the section.

The laboratory director asks you to work through the remaining tasks. Who will make each change? Who will check the finished work? Would you recommend starting on Monday?

## The people at the meeting

- **Hospital information technology (IT)** staff keep the network and shared hospital systems running.
- **The LIS team** adds the test to the LIS and checks the connections that carry orders and results between systems. Those connections are called interfaces.
- **Pathology informatics** helps chemistry decide how the test should be ordered and reported, using the laboratory's usual naming and reporting practices.
- **Clinical informatics** helps clinicians use the EHR to care for patients. That includes reviewing alerts and other functions that use laboratory results.
- **Laboratory operations** staff prepare the section to run the assay. They arrange procedures, training, supplies and shift coverage.
- **The pathologist or laboratory director** approves the test's order and complete report, including the report clinicians see in the EHR.

Your hospital may use different job titles. The LIS team may belong to pathology or hospital IT, and one person may do several of these jobs.

## What needs checking in the chart?

A report includes the test name, units and reference information as well as the result. Comments, flags and corrections must also reach the clinician. The laboratory checks the complete report in a specified part of the EHR. Other chart views may show only selected parts of it.

The EHR also has an alert that uses the troponin result with diagnoses and medications. This is **clinical decision support (CDS)**: software that uses patient information to help someone make a care decision. Pathology and clinical informatics need to review this alert with chemistry and the clinicians who receive it.

## What you will be able to do

- Assign the test build, report review and bench preparation to the people who do that work.
- Explain why the laboratory must check the complete report in the EHR.
- Choose who should lead when a report, trend graph or alert is wrong.
- Use the meeting notes to decide whether Monday's start should wait.
