# Follow one test from the LIS to the chart

## Who to meet

The laboratory director or section pathologist, and an LIS analyst who worked on a recent test build.

## Time

30–60 minutes. Ask them to pick the test before you meet.

## Steps

1. Ask the analyst to show you where the test name, units, reference interval and comments are entered in the LIS.
2. Look at one complete report in the LIS and in the part of the EHR the laboratory checks. Compare the two, including any correction.
3. Ask who tested that chart view and who signed off on the report. Find the saved test record or checklist with them.
4. Find one alert, trend graph or order set that uses the result. Ask who checks it when the assay or the report changes.
5. Ask the bench supervisor how staff on each shift learned to run and report the test.

## Bring back

A drawing of the route from analyzer to LIS to EHR. Beside each step, write the job title of the person who changes it and the person who checks it. Mark where the complete report is checked in the chart, and add one question you could not answer.

## Discuss with faculty

If the number reached the chart but the comment did not, who would you call first? Use your drawing to explain who would fix it and who would check the repaired report.

## Privacy

Look at records with your supervisor in the hospital's own systems. Bring back job titles and a description of where the reports appear, without patient names, identifiers or screenshots.
