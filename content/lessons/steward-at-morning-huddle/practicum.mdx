# Follow one test from the LIS to the chart

## Who to meet

Meet the laboratory director or section pathologist and an LIS analyst who worked on a recent test build.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes. Ask them to choose the test before you meet.

## Steps

1. Ask the analyst to show where the test name, units, reference information and comments are entered in the LIS.
2. View a complete report in the LIS and in the part of the EHR that the laboratory checks. Compare the two, including any correction.
3. Ask who tested that chart view and who signed off on the report. Find the saved test record or checklist with them.
4. Find one alert, trend graph or order set that uses the result. Ask who checks it when the assay or report changes.
5. Ask the bench supervisor how staff on each shift learned to run and report the test.

## Bring back

Draw the route from analyzer to LIS to EHR. Beside each step, name the job title of the person who changes it and the person who checks it. Add the location of the complete report in the chart and one question you could not answer.

## Discuss with faculty

If the number reached the chart but the comment was missing, who would you call first? Use your diagram to explain who would fix it and who would check the repaired report.

## Privacy

Review records with your supervisor in the hospital's approved systems. Bring back job titles and a description of the report locations, without patient names, identifiers or screenshots.
