## Debrief

**Informatics principle:** A valid calculation cannot rescue an invalid denominator.

During conversion, some arrival events moved from a structured timestamp into free text. The warehouse filtered the resulting null intervals, preferentially removing delayed manual accessions. The dashboard therefore converted incompleteness into apparent improvement.

The analyst and data engineer repair provenance and computation. Operations validates the workflow definition. Laboratory leadership approves the corrected metric and its residual-missingness display.
