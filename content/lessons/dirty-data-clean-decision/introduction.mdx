# Dirty Data, Clean Decision

> **Synthetic case:** On the day of an LIS upgrade, the STAT chemistry dashboard reports a 29% improvement. The bench remembers a difficult shift with long delays.

## Investigate before explaining

Follow the data from accession through extraction, transformation, aggregation, and display. Decide which quality dimensions are affected and whether the current metric is fit for operational use.

## Concept lens

Accuracy, completeness, validity, consistency, uniqueness, timeliness, and fitness for purpose answer different questions. A calculation can be arithmetically correct while its analytic population is wrong.
