### Review all objects affected by the assay change

The change record should identify the assay and report definitions, the separate
reflex rule, the authorized order type and population, downstream order codes,
duplicate checks, result status handling, and monitoring report. Test the lower
and upper branches, their exact boundaries, missing and preliminary results,
pending/completed FT4s, and cases outside the rule. Verify that an added order is
attached to the correct accession and reaches the intended worklist.

For affected production cases, chemistry staff and laboratory leadership review
which orders were added, whether testing has begun, and what action is appropriate.
Do not automatically cancel every FT4 or discard completed results. Document any
correction and communication. After the revised rule is approved, monitor added
orders per eligible TSH-with-reflex accession, duplicate orders, missed indicated
orders, and unresolved exceptions. Compare like populations and time periods.
