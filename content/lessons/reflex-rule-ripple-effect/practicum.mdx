# Apply it at your institution

## Who to meet

Meet the assay director and LIS analyst.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. List every result that depends on the reflex assay.
2. With your supervisor, select one approved record or workflow to examine.
3. Draw a reflex dependency map and define one quality indicator with a denominator.
4. Compare the proposed change with local policy and identify who would test it and who would approve it.

## Bring back

Bring a one-page annotated diagram, table or checklist that documents this task: draw a reflex dependency map and define one quality indicator with a denominator. Include one unresolved question and the person who can answer it.

## Discuss with faculty

What observation would change your conclusion? Explain why the shortcut “hide the unexpected comment in the chart only” could mislead the team.

## Privacy

Use only records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a workflow description without patient identifiers.
