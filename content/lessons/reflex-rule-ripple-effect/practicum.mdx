# Trace a local reflex order

## Who to meet

Meet an assay director and LIS or middleware analyst.

## Time

Allow 30–60 minutes with a supervisor.

## Steps

1. Select an approved reflex protocol. Identify the requested order type, population, initial test and possible added tests.
2. Find the written criteria and the saved rule. Ask which report definitions, result statuses and order codes the rule uses.
3. Follow an authorized accession through its initial result, rule evaluation and added or existing order.
4. Review test examples just below, at and above each boundary, plus missing results and existing follow-up orders.
5. Define a monitoring period and eligible population. Explain how the team would detect a missed addition, an unnecessary addition and a duplicate.

## Bring back

Bring a one-page diagram connecting the initial order, result, rule and follow-up test. Add a small table of expected actions and one monitoring measure with its numerator and denominator.

## Discuss with faculty

Which separate definition could be missed during an assay change? What would you check before acting on an order that the old rule already created?

## Privacy

Use records your supervisor authorizes you to review. Keep patient identifiers and screenshots within approved institutional systems; bring back a description without patient identifiers.
