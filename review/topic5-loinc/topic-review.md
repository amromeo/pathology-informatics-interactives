# Topic 5 teaching review

Reviewed October 22, 2026: introduction, concepts, bridge, debrief, faculty guidance, structured interaction data, and shared lesson rendering for all four Topic 5 lessons.

## Teaching sequence

1. Tracing a Missing Critical Flag: messages can arrive while a specific interpretation is lost. Preserve critical severity, distinguish observation identity from flags, test adjacent categories, and reconcile already affected reports.
2. Sending a Newborn Screening Report: identify the observation separately from its answer and patient identity. The first LOINC mention now explains the local-name problem and expands the acronym. Worklist routing remains distinct from receipt or completed clinical follow-up.
3. Why Laboratory Results Need LOINC: starts with differing local names and ambiguous identical names. Learners compare official term definitions, document two codes and six dimensions (seven comparison fields because property differs), distinguish other standards, assess mapping errors with accountable denominators, and reject a mismatched urine assignment.
4. Investigating Delayed Result Release: explicitly shifts from result meaning to currency, completeness, and linkage of operational records. Terminology does not repair stale data, omitted unfinished accessions, or inappropriate time intervals.
