# Sources checked for the workflow teaching revision

Checked November 1, 2026. Source links are also recorded in each lesson's manifest
and explained in its faculty guide. Case-specific routing, thresholds, order
criteria and expected actions remain unchanged.

- [CLSI AUTO12](https://clsi.org/shop/standards/auto12/): specimen-label content and
  placement. Used for optional faculty reading, without reproducing its text or
  presenting every label requirement as universal.
- [CLSI AUTO14](https://clsi.org/shop/standards/auto14/): two-dimensional barcoding
  in clinical and anatomic pathology. The public description addresses encoded
  content, printing, decoding and use. The lesson explains the connection between
  the scanned identifier, the record retrieved and the event recorded.
- [CLSI AUTO15](https://clsi.org/shop/standards/auto15/): design and validation of
  autoverification in specified laboratory disciplines. The public scope does
  not provide the lesson's serial-troponin thresholds or authorize local use.
- [Krasowski et al., 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4023033/): a
  published example of autoverification in core chemistry, including middleware
  and instrument/patient-result checks. The page initially returned a browser
  check; indexed primary-source text supplied its abstract and explanation.
  No rate or algorithm was imported into the case.
- [NIDDK, adult eGFR equations](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/research-funding/research-programs/kidney-clinical-research-epidemiology/laboratory/glomerular-filtration-rate-equations/adults):
  identifies age among the inputs to adult creatinine-based equations. Supports
  the missing-input example; this exercise does not implement an eGFR calculator.
- [American Thyroid Association, thyroid function tests](https://www.thyroid.org/thyroid-function-tests/):
  optional clinical context for TSH and FT4. The reflex thresholds and adult
  protocol remain the case laboratory's explicitly approved policy.

The existing Harrison/API source plan remains in place: session 3 for LIS
records and configuration, session 9 for validation and controlled changes, and
session 7 for reflex orders. The existing specimen and reflex "A closer look"
supplements and their conference attribution are preserved verbatim.
